DIEGO BILLS BEAUTY CARE WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: page structure and website content
- style.css: layout, colours, typography and responsive design
- script.js: mobile navigation and appointment form interaction
- images/hero.jpg: add your homepage hero background image here
- images/about.jpg: optional photo for the About section

HOW TO VIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep the files and images folder together.
3. Open index.html in a browser.

HERO IMAGE
Add your chosen photo to the images folder and name it exactly hero.jpg.
The hero section already points to images/hero.jpg. You can change the
background position in style.css under the .hero rule.

IMPORTANT
The appointment form is currently front-end only. It displays a confirmation
on the page but does not send or store bookings. To receive real appointments,
connect it to WhatsApp, email, a booking platform or a backend.
Review the service list and remove any service that is not actually offered.
Update contact details and social links once those details are available.
